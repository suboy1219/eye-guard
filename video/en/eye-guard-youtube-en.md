## Description

Staring at a screen all day? EyeGuard is a tiny eye-care app that lives in your Windows tray.

- 45 minutes of focus, then a 5-minute break, with a live countdown ring
- A full-screen rest overlay across every monitor; skipping unlocks after 10 seconds
- Optional 20-20-20 reminders: every 20 minutes, look 6 m away for 20 seconds
- Gamma-ramp blue-light filter that applies system-wide with negligible CPU cost
- One ~86 KB executable. No install, no camera, no screen capture, no ads

Free and open source. Double-click and go.
GitHub: https://github.com/suboy1219/eye-guard

Note: HDR mode, some discrete GPU drivers, and Remote Desktop may report success
while showing no visible blue-light effect. That is a system/driver limitation.
